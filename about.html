Philosophy

In the age of AI,
the human touch
matters more than ever.

In a world where algorithms churn out websites by the thousands, a different path gets chosen here: sitting with you, listening carefully, understanding what makes you you — your ambitions, your personality, your story — then building something that reflects exactly that.

Daniel studied at both ETH Zurich and the University of St. Gallen (HSG), a combination that gives him deep technical knowledge alongside a sharp sense for business strategy and effective marketing communication.

Unlike automatic AI generators that use predefined templates, each webpage is created from scratch. No templates. No shortcuts. Just deliberate, precise craft, designed to make you stand out, not blend in.

And when it's done? It's yours. Every file, every pixel, every line of code, everything transferred to you with full ownership. You're the owner. You're the king. Building the castle is simply the job.

Need to add news, update a section, or evolve your presence? Support is always available. Either hand the keys back for a small monthly fee, or update it yourself: the codebase is clean and readable by design. Either way, you're never locked in.

What's Different

Six principles,
never compromised.

Zero templates, ever

Every website starts at a blank canvas. Your site will never look like anyone else's, because it's built only for you.

You own the code

Handing over the keys means handing over everything. Full codebase. No subscriptions. No platform lock-in. It's yours outright.

Aligned to your identity

Before a single line gets written, getting to know you comes first. Your design reflects your personality, your voice, and the impression you want to make.

Detail-obsessed

Every micro-animation, every spacing decision, every font choice is intentional. Nothing ships without pride behind it.

Human, not algorithmic

AI can generate. It cannot understand. The slow, careful work that algorithms skip still gets done here, and it shows in every finished project.

Selective by design

Only a few projects are taken on at a time, because every client deserves full, undivided attention. Working together means getting all of it.
