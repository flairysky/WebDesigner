Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 28, 2026

Glowy Design Studio is committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how data is collected, used, and safeguarded when you visit this website or use its services.

1. Information Collected

The following types of information may be collected:

  • Contact information — name and email address submitted through the contact form.
  • Project details — information you voluntarily share about yourself and your project.
  • Usage data — anonymised analytics data (pages visited, time on site, referral source) collected to help improve this website.

2. How Your Information Is Used

Collected information is used to:

  • Respond to your enquiries and deliver the services offered.
  • Understand how this website is used and continuously improve it.
  • Communicate project updates and relevant information with active clients.

Your personal data is never sold, rented, or traded to third parties.

3. Analytics

Microsoft Clarity is used to understand how visitors interact with this website. Microsoft Clarity is a behavioural analytics tool that may record mouse movements, clicks, scrolls, and page navigation to help improve the content and usability of the site. This data is collected only with your consent, which you can grant or withdraw at any time via the cookie banner or the Cookie Settings link in the footer.

Microsoft may also use the data collected by Clarity for their own purposes in accordance with Microsoft's Privacy Statement. By accepting analytics cookies on this site, you agree that both Glowy Design Studio and Microsoft may collect and use this data.

4. Cookies

This website uses cookies to gather analytics data through Microsoft Clarity. On a first visit, consent is requested before any tracking cookies are set. You may:

  • Accept — analytics cookies are enabled and Microsoft Clarity will begin tracking your session.
  • Reject — no analytics cookies are set and Microsoft Clarity will not be loaded.

You can change your preference at any time using the Cookie Settings link in the footer of any page, which reopens this choice. You may also disable cookies entirely in your browser settings without affecting your ability to use the site. If you'd like any analytics data already collected about your visits to be deleted, reach out via the contact form and it will be removed.

5. Third-Party Services

The following third-party services may process data on behalf of Glowy Design Studio:

  • Microsoft Clarity — behavioural analytics. Data is processed by Microsoft in accordance with the Microsoft Privacy Statement. Clarity is only activated with your explicit consent.
  • Formspree — contact form processing. Formspree may store and process submitted contact data in accordance with their own privacy policy, available at formspree.io.

6. Data Retention

Contact form submissions are retained only as long as necessary to respond to your enquiry or fulfil the services provided. You may request deletion of your data at any time by reaching out.

7. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to access, correct, or delete personal data held about you. To exercise any of these rights, please reach out directly.

8. Security

Reasonable precautions are taken to protect your information. However, no method of internet transmission is 100% secure, and absolute security cannot be guaranteed.

9. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.

10. Contact

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please reach out via the contact form.
